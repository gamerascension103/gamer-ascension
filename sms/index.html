Ascension coaching reminders · operated by Jacob Salisbury

Inside Ascension: The Spire, three coach characters (Kael, Ilyra and Professor Strix) help people plan their day and follow through. People can choose to get their reminders as text messages, and separately as phone calls in the characters' AI-generated voices. Both are optional and off by default.

How people opt in

  1. In the Ascension app, open Settings, then Reminders.
  2. Turn on Text messages and enter a mobile number.
  3. Tick the box next to the consent statement below. The box starts unticked. Calls have their own separate box.
  4. Tap Save. A confirmation text arrives right away.

Stopping and help

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop all texts. Reply START to turn them back on.
  • Say "stop calling" on any call, or turn calls off in Settings, to stop calls.
  • Reply HELP to any text, or email support@ascension-1.live.
