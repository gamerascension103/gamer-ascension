How it works

1

Set your Power List

Each morning, pick the 3 to 5 actions that would make today a win. Workouts, habits and the to-dos that move your life forward.

2

Do it, level up

Finish them to earn XP, train your nine virtues and open the Daily Box. Progress over perfection: a missed day is never punished.

3

Climb the Spire

Your week powers dungeons, raids and Ascent, a puzzle climb where skill takes you as high as you can go.
