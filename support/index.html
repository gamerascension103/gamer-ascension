Quick answers

How do I stop text messages or calls?

Reply STOP to any text, or say "stop calling" on a call. You can also turn each one off in the app under Settings, Reminders. More about texts and calls.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the address on your account and ask for deletion. We delete your personal information within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I see what the coach characters remember about me?

Yes. In the app you can see, edit and delete everything they have noted from your conversations.

Is this medical advice?

No. Ascension offers fitness and wellness guidance, not medical care. Talk to a doctor before starting a new program. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (in the US).
